# 🌙 New Update: Dark Mode is here!

**Date:** June 4, 2026
**Audience:** Lead Gen team

Hey everyone! 👋

Quick one — the Call Coach side panel now has a **Dark Mode**, and it's live in the
latest extension update.

## What's new

- There's a new **moon icon** in the top-right of the panel header. Tap it and the
  whole panel switches to a sleek dark theme. Tap it again to go back to light.
- A little **"Dark" badge** shows up in the header so you always know which mode
  you're in.
- Whatever you pick **sticks** — set it once and it stays that way every time you
  open the panel.
- The Simple.Biz logo automatically turns white in dark mode so it still looks crisp.

## Why you'll like it

Long calling sessions are a lot easier on the eyes in dark mode, especially later in
the day or in low light. Nothing about your calls, transcripts, or coaching tips
changes — it's purely a comfort upgrade. 😎

## How to get it

It rolls out automatically with the next extension update — no action needed on your
end. Once your extension updates, just look for the moon icon and give it a tap.

Try it out and let us know what you think!

Happy Call Coaching everyone! 🎉
